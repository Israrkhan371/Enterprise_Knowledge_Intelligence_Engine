# Company Policy - Remote Work

**Document type:** Company Policy
**Owner:** Ezitech People Operations

## Scope

Applies to all employees and interns, including those working on EEF
case studies remotely.

## Eligibility for interns

Interns may complete their case study fully remotely unless their
specific track requires on-site equipment access (rare — flagged
explicitly in the case study document if so). Remote interns have the
same mentor check-in cadence and deliverable expectations as on-site
interns; there is no reduced scope for remote participation.

## Working hours

- Interns are expected to be reachable during a consistent 4-hour
  overlap window with their mentor's working hours, agreed during
  mentor onboarding.
- Outside the overlap window, interns may work flexibly, provided
  deliverables and check-in commitments are met on schedule.

## Equipment and access

Remote interns request repository, tool, and infrastructure access
through the standard process (see SOP - Access Request Process); no
separate remote-specific access tier exists.

## Communication expectations

- Daily async status updates in the team's working channel during active
  case-study weeks.
- Camera-on for weekly mentor check-ins, unless connectivity prevents it.
