# SOP - Access Request Process

**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure
**Owner:** Ezitech IT & Security
**Applies to:** Interns and mentors requesting access to internal
repositories, tools, and infrastructure.

## Purpose

Ensures access to internal systems is granted deliberately, tracked, and
scoped to what's actually needed for the requester's current case study —
not handed out ad hoc over Slack DMs.

## Who can request access

Any active intern or mentor may request access. Requests on behalf of
someone else (e.g. a mentor requesting for their whole team) must name
each individual explicitly.

## How to request access

1. Submit an access request via the IT ticket form, specifying:
   - The system/repo/tool needed (e.g. "EKIE GitHub repo, read/write")
   - The business reason (e.g. "assigned to AI-007 case study")
   - Requested duration (default: length of the current case study)
2. Your mentor approves the request first, confirming it matches your
   assignment.
3. IT & Security provisions access, typically within 1 business day for
   standard tooling (GitHub, Slack, LMS) and up to 3 business days for
   production or customer-data systems.

## Access review

All intern access is reviewed and revoked automatically at the end of
their case study unless explicitly extended by a mentor. Mentors' access
is reviewed quarterly.
