# SOP - Escalation Procedures

**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure
**Owner:** Ezitech Engineering Enablement

## Purpose

Defines what to do when something in an internship case study is stuck —
an unresponsive mentor, a blocked deliverable, or a disagreement about
scope — so problems get resolved quickly instead of silently stalling a
team's progress.

## Escalation triggers

- A mentor has not responded to a check-in request for more than 48 hours.
- An intern team is blocked on a decision only a mentor or manager can make.
- A case-study deadline is at risk and the team needs a scope adjustment.
- A conflict between team members that the team cannot resolve on its own.

## Escalation path

1. **Step 1 - Mentor.** Raise the issue with your assigned mentor first,
   in the mentor Slack channel or during a check-in.
2. **Step 2 - Mentor's manager.** If the mentor is unresponsive for more
   than 48 hours (see SOP - Mentor Onboarding), escalate to the mentor's
   manager, cc'ing Engineering Enablement.
3. **Step 3 - Engineering Enablement.** If Step 2 doesn't resolve the
   issue within 2 business days, Engineering Enablement takes over
   directly and may reassign the case study to a different mentor.

## Documentation

Every escalation past Step 1 must be logged (channel, date, reason) so
Engineering Enablement can spot recurring bottlenecks across cohorts.
