# SOP - Mentor Onboarding

**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure
**Owner:** Ezitech Engineering Enablement
**Applies to:** New mentors joining an active internship cohort

## Purpose

Defines the steps a new mentor must complete before being assigned interns,
so mentor guidance stays consistent across cohorts and interns get a
uniform onboarding experience regardless of which mentor they're assigned.

## Procedure

1. **Access provisioning.** Engineering Enablement grants the mentor
   access to the LMS mentor dashboard, the shared case-study repository,
   and the mentor Slack channel within 1 business day of the mentor
   accepting the role.
2. **Shadow session.** The new mentor shadows one full intern check-in
   run by an existing mentor before taking on their own interns.
3. **Case-study familiarization.** The mentor reads the case study
   packet(s) for their assigned track (e.g. AI-007) and completes the
   mentor quiz confirming they understand the evaluation criteria.
4. **First assignment.** The mentor is assigned 1-2 interns initially,
   scaling up after their first successful 2-week checkpoint review.
5. **Escalation path acknowledgement.** The mentor confirms they've read
   SOP - Escalation Procedures and know who to contact if an intern is
   unresponsive or a case study is at risk.

## Response-time expectations

Mentors are expected to respond to intern check-ins within 48 hours. If a
mentor is unresponsive for more than 48 hours, the intern (or the mentor's
manager) should follow the escalation path in SOP - Escalation Procedures,
which routes the case to the mentor's manager and, if unresolved, to
Engineering Enablement directly.
